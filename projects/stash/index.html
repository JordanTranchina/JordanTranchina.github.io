Stash

A self-hosted read-it-later app.

Stash is a personal replacement for Pocket and Instapaper. Save an article from your browser or your phone, and Stash keeps a clean, readable copy in your own library. Read it later on any device, or let Stash turn your saves into a short AI podcast you can play in any podcast app.

Open Stash View the code on GitHub

What Stash does

  • Save from anywhere. Use the Chrome or Firefox extension, a bookmarklet, the share sheet on iOS and Android, or paste a link in the web app.
  • Read without clutter. Stash pulls out the article text so you get a clean reading view with themes, font sizes, search, folders, and tags.
  • Keep highlights. Import Kindle highlights and CSV exports from other read-it-later apps.
  • Save videos too. Save a YouTube link and Stash keeps its transcript next to your articles.
  • Listen instead of read. Once a day, Stash can turn your recent saves into a two-host AI podcast, published as a private RSS feed.
  • Works offline. The web app installs to your home screen and queues saves until you are back online.

Signing in with Google

Stash uses Google sign-in only to create your account and keep your library private to you. Stash asks Google for your name, email address, and profile picture, and nothing else. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

Stash is invite-only. It is a personal project run by Jordan Tranchina for a small group of invited testers, and it is not a commercial product. Your data is never sold or used for advertising. The full details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
